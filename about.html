ABOUT US

Welcome To SAMRI GROUP LTD

SAMRI GROUP LTD is a diversified business group registered in the Republic of Malawi, with its head office in Lilongwe. The Group is focused on delivering practical, professional and dependable commercial solutions across logistics and transportation, general trading, procurement and general supplies, warehousing and distribution, and agriculture and commodity trade.

Discover Our Services

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted diversified business group, recognised for excellence, integrity, innovation and sustainable business growth across Africa and international markets.

Our Mission

To deliver quality, affordable and reliable business solutions through professionalism, innovation, strategic partnerships and responsive, customer-focused service. We aim to create lasting value by understanding client requirements, coordinating appropriate resources and maintaining dependable business relationships.

Strategic Partnerships
